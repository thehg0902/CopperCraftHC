Commercial

Light Commercial HVAC

We service and install heating and cooling for offices, shops, restaurants and other small commercial spaces — including rooftop units and back-room equipment.

What is different about commercial

  • Comfort affects staff, customers and stock.
  • Commercial units run far more hours than a home system.
  • The rooftop unit nobody looks at gets noticed on the day it quits.

Helpful to know

  • Longer run-hours mean filters and belts need a tighter schedule than at home.
  • Seasonal service can be planned around your business hours, not through them.
  • Knowing the unit's make, model and age speeds up any service call — a photo of the data plate is enough.
  • Hot or cold complaints from one area are an early warning, not a nuisance — they usually mean something specific.

Also serving commercial HVAC in Vaughan and Newmarket.

Common Questions

Do you work on rooftop units?

Yes. Packaged rooftop units are common on storefronts and small commercial buildings because they keep mechanical equipment out of leasable floor space.

My unit is leased. Who is responsible for the repair?

That is between you and your landlord and it varies by lease. It is worth establishing before the visit, so nobody is surprised by who receives the invoice.

Can you service equipment outside business hours?

We do not run a 24/7 service. For commercial work where a daytime shutdown is a problem, tell us when you book and we will be straight with you about what we can and cannot schedule.
