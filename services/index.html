Not Sure Which One You Need?

Most people book by symptom rather than by service name. That is fine — describe what is happening and we will work out the rest.

  • Something has stopped working

    Start with the repair pages. The diagnosis comes before any recommendation, and you get the findings before work goes ahead.

    • No heat, short cycling, cold air — furnace repair.
    • Running but not cooling, weak airflow, ice on the line — air-conditioning repair.

  • It still works, but it is old

    Age alone is not a reason to replace. It is a reason to get it assessed while the decision is still yours to plan rather than forced at the worst moment.

    • Furnace installation and air-conditioning installation.
    • Worth comparing against a heat pump on any full replacement.

  • One room is never comfortable

    Usually a distribution or controls problem rather than a capacity one. A bigger system rarely fixes it.

    • No ductwork to that room — ductless systems.
    • The house fights its own thermostat — thermostats and controls.

  • The air itself feels wrong

    Not a temperature problem. A tightly built house has nowhere to put stale air or the moisture that comes with it.

    • Condensation on the windows, stuffy bedrooms, air that feels stale — HRV systems.
    • The house goes bone-dry every January instead — ERV systems.

  • Nothing is wrong yet

    The cheapest appointment you will book all year, and the one that prevents the expensive one.

    • Before the season, not during it — preventative maintenance.
    • Also water heaters and light commercial HVAC.
