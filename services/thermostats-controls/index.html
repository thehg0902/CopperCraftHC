Thermostats and Controls

We install and troubleshoot thermostats and controls — from simple programmable units to smart thermostats — wired correctly and set up for the system you actually have.

Why the controls matter

  • The thermostat is the brain of the whole system.
  • Poor placement or the wrong setup causes uneven temperatures and wasted run-time.
  • No amount of new equipment fixes a controls problem.

Helpful to know

  • Many smart thermostats need a C-wire for power — worth checking before you buy one.
  • A programmed schedule beats big manual setbacks you have to remember.
  • If you have a heat pump, keep setbacks mild — deep ones can trigger expensive backup heat.
  • Keep the thermostat away from direct sun, drafts and supply vents; it can only react to the air right around it.

Common Questions

Will a smart thermostat work with my system?

Most modern systems yes, but it depends on the wiring at the furnace and whether a common wire is present. Multi-stage and heat pump systems need a thermostat that actually supports them, which not all consumer models do.

One floor is always wrong. Will a new thermostat fix it?

On its own, usually not. A single thermostat can only measure where it is. Zoning or airflow balancing is normally the real fix, and controls are one part of that.

Do I need a C wire for a smart thermostat?

Most need a common wire for continuous power. Where one is not present at the furnace, an adapter or running a new wire are both options. It is worth checking before buying a thermostat rather than after.
