Maintenance

Preventative Maintenance

A seasonal maintenance visit inspects, cleans and tests your system — furnace in the fall, air conditioning in the spring — so worn parts are caught before they fail.

Why it pays for itself

  • Equipment rarely fails at a convenient time — it fails at the season's peak, when it is working hardest.
  • Regular service keeps small issues small.
  • Many manufacturers expect annual service records to keep the warranty intact.

Helpful to know

  • Book the furnace check in fall and the A/C check in spring, ahead of the seasonal rush.
  • Change or check filters between visits — the single most useful thing a homeowner can do.
  • Keep about 60 cm of clearance around outdoor equipment, all year round.
  • Mention new noises or smells early, and note when they started — that history is genuinely useful.

Also serving seasonal maintenance in Markham, Aurora and Newmarket.

Common Questions

How often should the system be serviced?

Once a year for a furnace or an air conditioner, and both if you have both. Heat pumps run year-round, so they benefit from being checked more like twice.

When is the best time to book it?

Before the season starts, not during it. Booking a furnace check in September gets you a far better window than calling on the first cold night in November, when everyone else is calling too.

What actually gets checked on a maintenance visit?

Combustion and safety controls, the flame sensor and igniter, airflow and filter condition, the blower, refrigerant charge and coil condition on cooling equipment, and the thermostat calibration. The point is catching the small fault before it becomes the emergency.
