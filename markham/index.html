What Markham Houses Actually Need

Markham is not one housing stock. What a service call looks like depends entirely on which part of it you live in.

  • Older Unionville and Milliken Mills: ductwork that grew, not ductwork that was designed

    • Homes here predate the subdivisions around them.
    • Duct runs were usually extended over the years rather than laid out as a whole.
    • The result is uneven rooms that look like an equipment fault and are not one.

  • Cornell and Berczy Village: a whole street ageing at once

    • Large numbers of houses built within a few years of each other.
    • A furnace from the late 1990s or early 2000s is now where parts get scarce and efficiency has fallen behind.
    • The question stops being can this be fixed and becomes is fixing it the better value this year.
    • We give you the arithmetic, not the sales pitch.

  • Cornell specifically: nowhere to put the condenser

    • Rear-lane layouts and narrow side yards leave very little clearance.
    • The unit needs airflow room, service access, and distance from a bedroom window.
    • Worth solving before equipment is ordered, not after it arrives.

  • Markham Centre: the equipment has to fit the room

    • Townhomes and low-rise units with small mechanical rooms.
    • Sizing is constrained by the space as much as by the heat load.

Markham Questions We Get Asked

Do you charge extra to come out to Markham?

Markham is inside our regular service area, so it is handled the same way as a Richmond Hill call. Any diagnostic fee is explained when you book, before a technician is dispatched.

My Cornell house has almost no side yard. Can a condenser still go in?

Usually yes, but placement needs to be looked at before equipment is ordered. The unit needs clearance for airflow and room for a technician to service it, and it is worth keeping it away from bedroom windows.

The whole street is replacing furnaces. Does mine need it too?

Not necessarily. Houses built in the same year do not fail on the same schedule. We inspect the system and tell you what we actually find, whether that supports a replacement or not.

What We Will Not Tell You

We will not tell you a system needs replacing when it does not, and we will not quote a Markham price over the phone before anyone has looked at the equipment. The diagnosis comes first, then the options, then your decision.
