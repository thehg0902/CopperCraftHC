What to expect

A Straightforward Service Experience From the First Call

Know what happens before, during and after your CopperCraft service visit.

Call (647) 250-6072 Book a Service Visit
  1. 1

    Tell Us What Is Happening

    Call or submit a request and briefly describe the heating or cooling problem. You do not need to diagnose it yourself.

  2. 2

    Choose an Available Time

    CopperCraft will help arrange a service window based on availability and urgency.

  3. 3

    The System Is Inspected

    The equipment is checked to identify the actual cause of the issue.

  4. 4

    You Receive a Clear Explanation

    The problem, available options and recommended next step are explained in plain language.

  5. 5

    You Decide How to Move Forward

    You receive the information needed to make a comfortable decision before additional work proceeds.

  6. 6

    The Work Is Completed and Tested

    Approved work is completed carefully, and the system is checked before the visit is finished.

No Pressure to Approve Work

The purpose of the visit is to identify the problem and explain your options. You should understand what is being recommended before deciding how to proceed.
