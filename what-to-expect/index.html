What a No-Heat Call Actually Involves

Heater repair in Richmond Hill is mostly diagnosis. The repair itself is often quick once the cause is known — finding the cause is the work.

  • The usual causes of a no-heat call

    Each produces a different pattern of behaviour, which is why the first question is what the system is doing, not which part you would like replaced.

    • A failed igniter, or a flame sensor coated in residue.
    • A pressure switch reacting to a blocked intake or exhaust.
    • A thermostat that has lost power or calibration.
    • A limit switch tripping because airflow is restricted.
    • A blower motor drawing too much current.

  • Short cycling is the most misread symptom

    Lighting, running briefly, shutting down again. It looks like a dying furnace and very often is not.

    • A clogged filter will do it.
    • So will a return duct that was never sized for the equipment.
    • Replace the furnace and the new one short cycles identically. That is not a repair worth paying for.

  • Worth checking before you call. All free.

    None of it costs anything, and occasionally one of them is the whole problem.

    • Thermostat batteries.
    • The furnace power switch. It looks like a light switch and gets turned off by accident.
    • The breaker.
    • Is the filter visibly grey?
    • Through the inspection window, does it attempt to ignite at all? That one detail narrows the diagnosis considerably.

  • This one is not a diagnostic question

    If you smell gas, or a carbon-monoxide alarm sounds, leave the house first and call your gas utility from outside. That comes before calling us or anyone else.

Repair or Replace

The honest version of a question that gets answered dishonestly a lot.

  • Repair usually wins when

    • The failed component is a normal wear item.
    • The rest of the system is sound.
    • There are still years of reasonable service left in the unit.

  • Replacement starts making sense when

    • The equipment is old enough that parts are getting scarce.
    • The efficiency gap has grown enough to show on the bill.
    • The repair costs a meaningful fraction of a new system anyway.

  • Nobody can tell you which over the phone

    And we will not try. The system gets inspected, you get the findings, you decide.

  • If cost is the obstacle

    Financing is a separate conversation we are happy to have. An option, not a push. You can also see the full range on the services page, including furnace repair and preventative maintenance, which is the cheapest way to avoid this call entirely.
