Before You Call

None of this is required. It just tends to make the visit shorter.

  • Tell us what it is doing, not what you think is broken

    That pattern is what points a technician at the right part of the system before anyone opens a panel.

    • Does the furnace try to ignite and fail, or not respond at all?
    • Is the air cold, or just weak?
    • Is there a new noise, and roughly when did it start?

  • Have the make and age if you happen to know it

    Do not go hunting for it. If you do not know, we will find it.

    • Usually on a label inside the furnace door.
    • Or on the side of the outdoor condenser.

  • Urgent? Call. Do not use the form.

    (647) 250-6072 reaches us directly during business hours.

  • We are not a 24/7 service

    Outside business hours a message is answered the next working day rather than overnight. We would rather tell you that than leave you waiting for a callback that was never coming.
