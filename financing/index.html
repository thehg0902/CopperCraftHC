Let us clear something up

“I Thought I Would Have to Pay Thousands Up Front”

It is the most common reason people delay replacing an aging or failing system — the assumption that the only option is one large payment. That is simply not the case.

Just as most people finance a car rather than paying the full price at once, a heating or cooling system can be spread over time. You get a safe, efficient home now, and pay in predictable monthly amounts.

And in true CopperCraft fashion, financing is an option, not a push. If a repair makes more sense than a replacement, we will tell you that first.

How Financing Works

No jargon and no pressure. Four steps from “that is too expensive right now” to a comfortable home.

  1. 1

    Pick Your System

    We recommend the right-sized furnace, air conditioner or heat pump for your home — nothing oversold.

  2. 2

    Choose a Monthly Plan

    Select a payment length that fits your budget instead of one large upfront cost.

  3. 3

    Apply for Approval

    A short application through our financing partner, usually answered quickly.

  4. 4

    Stay Comfortable

    We install and test the system now. You pay in predictable monthly amounts.

Common questions

Financing Questions, Answered

Does applying commit me to anything?

No. Asking about financing or submitting an application does not obligate you to any purchase or service.

Can I still choose to repair instead?

Absolutely. Financing is only an option. If a repair is the smarter call, we will say so.

Who provides the financing?

Financing is arranged through Financeit, a third-party lending partner. The application and any sensitive details are handled securely on their platform — not collected on this website.

What do I need to apply?

Basic contact information to start the conversation. The lender guides you through the secure application from there.
