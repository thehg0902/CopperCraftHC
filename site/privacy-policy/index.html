Privacy Policy

How we handle the information you send us through this website.

Draft for review. This page was prepared as a starting point and is not legal advice. Have it reviewed before the site goes live.

What we collect

If you complete the contact form on this site, we collect the information you choose to enter: your name, phone number, email address, service address, a description of the problem, your preferred contact method and your preferred day or time.

We do not ask for payment details through this website.

Why we collect it

We use this information only to respond to your enquiry, arrange a service visit and discuss the work you asked about. We do not sell it, rent it or trade it.

How it is stored and shared

Enquiries are sent to CopperCraft Heating & Cooling and handled by the people who arrange and carry out the work. We share your details with third parties only where it is necessary to deliver the service you requested, or where we are required to by law.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set advertising cookies and does not run third-party analytics tracking. Pages that embed a Google Map may set cookies controlled by Google under its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond and to maintain a record of work carried out, then dispose of them securely.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact us at info@coppercraft.ca or (647) 250-6072.
